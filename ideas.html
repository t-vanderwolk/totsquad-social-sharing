How we work

Small habits that keep ideas moving.

    Got a “what if?”

    It doesn’t need to be perfect. Pitch it and the Squad can help shape it.

    • Pitch itGive it a working title and a sentence or two. That’s enough.
    • It shows up hereYour pitch lands in the Idea Bank right away, marked “Just pitched,” and Sarah and Taylor review it.
    • Shape it togetherAny creator can open an idea and tap Edit to add steps, notes or a lead.
    • Someone leads itOne lead per idea. When it’s ready, it moves to Casting on the Creators Club page.
