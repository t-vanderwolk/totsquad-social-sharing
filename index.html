Creators Club

By application
Who
Specialists who want ongoing collaboration and accountability.
Join
Apply. A coordinator replies by email or Instagram DM.
Expected
1 feed post a month and 1 Instagram story a week, meaningful engagement with fellow members, and a heads-up when you need a break.
You get
A brief every other week, the private Creator Circle in Disco, the Idea Bank and approved B-roll originals.

Membership is by application only. Joining the chat, answering a casting call or sending B-roll doesn’t sign you up. The Creators Club is voluntary and separate from your role as a specialist.

    Questions

    Where does everything happen?

    Instagram for conversation. Disco for Social & Sharing (every Hub specialist) and the Creator Circle (members). This site for casting calls, B-roll and tools.

    What happens after I answer a casting call?

    Your clip goes to the call’s lead and the team, who reach out if they need anything. Not every clip makes the final Reel.

    There’s a place for your voice.

    You don’t need a perfect pitch or a polished video. Start with something you care about.
