    Before you upload

    • Film vertical9:16, steady, in good light. Reels crop everything else.
    • Keep it short3 to 15 seconds, with no talking or music, so anyone can reuse it.
    • Protect privacyGet permission from anyone on camera. Keep babies’ faces, client names, registries and order details out.
    • Send it with Add your clipsChoose your name and your clips are filed in your own folder automatically.
    • Name it clearlycategory_what, for example strollers_one-hand-fold.mov.
