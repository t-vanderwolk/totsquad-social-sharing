# Tot Squad Hub — working brand and styling rules

Source: https://totsquad.com/ and its published stylesheet, inspected November 16, 2026. These are implementation rules for this hub, derived from the live website, not a claim to be an approved corporate brand manual.

## Identity
- Product name: Tot Squad Hub. Section: Social & Sharing. Audience: Tot Squad specialists.
- Use the original Tot Squad logo from the official website. Preserve its proportions and colors; never recreate it as text or add a substitute emblem.
- Keep clear space around the logo. The hub label is secondary to the logo.
- Use “Tot Squad” as two words in copy. Avoid invented taglines or presenting sample content as a live program.

## Visual system
- Primary plum: #875782. Primary headings: #3B2139.
- Teal accent: #6FC9BA. Accessible dark teal: #477F76.
- Linen: #F8F1E9. Peach: #FEE2D5. Soft lavender surface: #F3EAF1.
- Borders: #E3D4E1. Body copy: #4B3A49. White and near-white keep layouts open.
- Fraunces Soft 800 for prominent headings; Figtree 400–700 for body, labels and controls. Use Georgia and Arial as fallbacks.
- Plum pill-shaped primary buttons with white text. Pale secondary buttons with dark text. Never use white body text on pale teal or peach.
- Rounded 18–24px cards, restrained borders, generous whitespace. Use consistent spacing in multiples of 4px.
- Four action cards use lavender, pale teal, peach and linen in that order. Preserve a consistent icon, title, description and action hierarchy.
- Original logo and fonts are referenced from the official site's asset hosts; fallback fonts preserve usability if those hosts are unavailable.

## Voice
Warm, practical, expert and reassuring. Speak with families, not at them. Use inclusive language and respect different parenting experiences. Prefer one clear, useful takeaway. Avoid fear, shame, inflated claims, invented endorsements and promises of perfect outcomes.

## Interaction and accessibility
- Maintain the four distinct workflows and their separate local drafts.
- State plainly when work is a draft, sample, or manually shared. An outbound Disco link is not a submission integration.
- Use visible labels, semantic controls, keyboard focus indicators and accessible dialogs. Respect reduced motion.
- Keep type readable on mobile; collapse multi-column layouts before content becomes cramped.
- Preserve creator credit, captions, image descriptions and privacy reminders in the content workflow.
- No real member identities, live deadlines or casting calls without supplied source data.

## Future updates
Update CSS tokens rather than choosing new ad hoc colors. Use approved supplied brand assets or guidelines in preference to these website-derived rules when available. Check desktop and mobile layouts and the four workflows before publishing.
